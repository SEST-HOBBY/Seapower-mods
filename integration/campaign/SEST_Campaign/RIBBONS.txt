SEST R12 - RIBBONS AND NATIONAL AWARDS
10 November 2026 | SEST-DEV

WORLD OPERATIONS - F11 > SERVICE
25 fictional world awards now recognise manual battle service, distinct
campaign battle days, theatre experience and successful Admiralty duties.
Original awards and v1 service records are preserved. New task records use
separate compatible event keys and never inflate manual battle or USD totals.

Manual battle awards: 3 / 10 / 20 / 50 battles; watch awards: battles on
5 / 15 distinct campaign days; theatre service/veteran: 2 / 5 positioned
battles in each of six theatres; expeditionary/global command: service in
3 / all 6 theatres. These are evidenced service milestones, not assumed
continuous deployments or certificates of unrecorded combat performance.

Admiralty awards: 5 completed accepted orders; 3 different completed order
kinds; 3 convoy-cover, shadowing or route-clearance orders for each specialist
ribbon. Failed, cancelled, declined, lapsed, unaccepted, other-side and moot
outcomes are excluded. Native task conditions and rewards remain in effect.
Ribbons add no renown, credits or supplies. Auto-resolved battles do not
qualify for manual battle awards; task awards use their own native outcomes.

AUTHORED CAMPAIGNS - TASK FORCE SERVICE RECORD
The four original completion ribbons remain. Eight new fictional operation
ribbons recognise SW05, SW08, SR12, TS09, RL03, RL05, SL04 and SL06 after the
native required mission result. They do not claim zero losses or perfect ROE.

Southern Watch and Southern Reach keep Australia as the default commander
nation. New commanders may also choose US through the existing onboarding
screen. Committed commanders and national discounts retain native behaviour.
The fleet/scenario stays the same; national awards use the committed nation.

Six real-award representations use national selectors and modern scenario
citations. Their names/art refer to real awards; these are fictional-operation
qualifications in the game, not actual government honours or declarations:

- Australian Distinguished Service Cross: Australia; SW12 / TS09.
- Australian Operational Service Medal - Indo-Pacific: Australia; SR10.
  The authored assignment begins 6 December 2028; SR10 is 6 January 2029,
  over 30 campaign days later. Real eligibility also requires a declared
  qualifying operation. The SEST operation is fictional.
- Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal: US; SW05 / SR04.
- Navy and Marine Corps Commendation Medal: US; SW08 / SR12.
- Navy Unit Commendation: US; SW12 / TS09; unit recipient.
- Meritorious Unit Commendation: US; TS12; unit recipient.

Native completion and required-result checks guard mission grants. Native
service records retain citations and repeated-award counts. Existing earned
IDs are retained. Chinese and Philippine campaigns receive their own fictional
operation awards, without importing an unrelated country's national awards.

ART AND SOURCE NOTES
The AOSM-IP ribbon is Nford24's CC0 artwork, copied without modification.
Other real-award images reference installed Sea Power assets. Fictional SEST
strips are code-drawn. Campaign art/ribbons/SOURCES.txt provides credits.
Official criteria and scenario implementation are documented in the release
review; the citations identify the fictional scenario qualification.

PC CHECKS
Load an existing world and check Service progress, wrapping and scrolling.
Complete an accepted task, return to the map and compare task/battle counts.
Check a new Australian and US commander, native ribbon slots and citations.
The previously accepted opening brief, Combat Costs sidebar and direct F11
remain. Existing saves are preserved. New data applies through Prepare-PC;
starting a new commander is needed to choose another nationality.
