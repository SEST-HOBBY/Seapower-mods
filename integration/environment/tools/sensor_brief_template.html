    How to read this

    Game What Sea Power itself applies, from its own files: passive ambient 60 dB plus 5 dB per sea state, plus 15 dB in rain; Fog below 1 km; convergence zones at 30 and 60 nm wherever the water is deeper than 3000 ft. The game works out its own thermal layer and surface duct.

    Real world What the real sea and air would do here. The simulator does not model it; use it for planning and briefings.

    Untested Sonar values a per-mission override could carry. Nothing writes them until the PC test in SENSOR-PC-TESTS.md passes.
