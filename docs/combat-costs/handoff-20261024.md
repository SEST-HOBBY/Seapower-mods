## Accounting limits

Fitted platform models exclude separately counted ammunition, aircraft, removable pods, fuel and cargo. Pods/tanks price hardware; rounds are separate. People and visual/transfer processes are explicitly non-procured; infantry equipment is material only. Virtual deck stock pools are points, so stay `+ ?`. Older saves cannot recover absent magazines. Crew, repairs, damage-short-of-loss, operating fuel burn and unrepresented cargo are outside this material-loss account. A native loss not registered at all cannot be invented. The original F11 mission tab keeps the author's scope; detailed SEST views and the campaign debrief cover the additions. Campaign totals cover completed missions once, not every failed/retried attempt. The Situation tab is unchanged.
