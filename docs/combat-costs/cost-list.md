# SEST combat cost list

Research reviewed 23 October 2026. Generated by `integration/combat-costs/build_pack.py` from the cost builder (`integration/common/costs.py`): the Combat Cost Tracker's captured catalogs, SEST's reviewed mappings and researched estimates (`integration/combat-costs/mappings.json`), the research record (`integration/combat-costs/research.json`) and SEST_Campaign's built missions and rosters. Do not edit it by hand: `tools/build_all.py` rebuilds it after the campaign. `cost-list.csv` beside it holds the same rows.

- **Some sources could not be read.** This environment's network policy blocked them (proxy 403 and DNS failures). No price that rests on such a page is shipped; the figures are listed under Gaps as candidates.
- **Researched estimates are SEST's**, not the tracker author's. Each is labelled with its method, year and sources, below and in the comment above its section in the shipped catalog.
- **Historical** is the tracker's rounded service-entry-era USD (`WeaponCosts`, `VesselCosts`, `AircraftCosts`). **Current** is the explicit `CurrentYearCost` in 2026 USD; it never falls back to the historical price. Ships are priced fitted, without ammunition or embarked aircraft. Unknown is never free, and purchase points are never converted to USD.
- **Price from**: `tracker catalog` (the author's entry), `inline in unit file`, `SEST mapping from <id>` (an author price copied exactly from that entry), `SEST researched estimate`, or `unknown` with the reason.
- Aircraft have no tracker category; the tracker splits planes and helicopters itself.
