# World Sandbox: real P-8A Poseidons, PC check (26 October 2026)

**Status: built and checked from source; not yet built on the PC or seen in Sea Power.** Never overwrite an existing save.

## What changed

Both World Sandbox campaigns, the original (`sest.world_sandbox_2028`) and the Regional Expansion (`sest.world_sandbox_2028.regional`), placed twelve US Navy P-8A in six air forces using Red Storm Arsenal's `usn_p_8a`. That unit is named a P-8A Poseidon but draws the P-3C Orion model (`ResourcesFolder=aircraft/usn_p-3c/`), as the 25 October Southern Cross playtest showed for Kiwi 01.

They now use U.S. Navy 2027 Capabilities' `usn_p8_2027` (3606774881), which draws `P8_Poseidon.obj` and has US Navy squadrons. Its model folder, `aircraft/P8_Poseidon/Upgrade/`, comes from a second mod, Boeing P-8 Poseidon (3602046770), so both must be in the load order; both are in the SEST order today. The World Sandbox builder makes the swap (`WORLD_UNIT_SUBSTITUTIONS` in `integration/world-sandbox/build_dynamic_campaign.py`, from PR #47). Nothing else changes:

- **Same places, same numbers.** Jacksonville 4, Muharraq (Bahrain) 2, Camp Lemonnier (Djibouti) 1, Sigonella 3, Souda Bay 1 and the US detachment at Evenes 1.
- **Same price.** 89, the value of a maritime patrol aircraft, as before.
- **The research is unchanged.** The register still names the unit its sources gave, and `integration/world-sandbox/draft/conversion.json` records each row as "2028 Poseidon model corrected from usn_p_8a (P-3 geometry)".
- **Other campaigns are unchanged.** Southern Watch and the other SEST campaigns, and the upstream mods, are not touched.
- **One fewer purchase.** The US Navy buy list no longer offers the P-3-bodied copy, so both campaigns list 1,560 purchases instead of 1,561. The other P-8s stay on sale: Boeing P-8 Poseidon's `usn_p8` ("P-8A Poseidon") and US Naval Aviation's `usn_p-8a` ("P-8A").
- **New force names.** The six forces take the unit's own name, so they read, for example, "Naval Air Station Jacksonville P-8A Poseidon (2027)". The Evenes detachment, until now "Evenes Air Station P-8A Poseidon 2" beside Norway's six `usn_p8`, becomes "Evenes Air Station P-8A Poseidon (2027)".
- **Force totals are unchanged.** 267 in the original and 293 in the Regional Expansion.

Only a new campaign gets the Poseidons. An old save still holds the `usn_p_8a` aircraft it started with. Red Storm Arsenal still provides that unit, so the aircraft can load, but the campaign file no longer prices or sells it, as already happened to the renamed HH-60H and Chilean frigate. How Dynamic Campaign Mod treats that in a save is untested; step 7 checks it on a copy.

Also in this build, with no effect in game: building the World Sandbox a second time no longer fails. Until now, only `tools/build_all.py --from-scratch` built it (see `docs/world-sandbox/DYNAMIC_CAMPAIGN_ADAPTER.md`, Mapping rules).

## On the PC

1. Start the original **World Sandbox** as a **new** campaign. START CAMPAIGN reaches the strategic map with no error.
2. Find a US Navy P-8 force, for example at Jacksonville or Sigonella. The aircraft is a Boeing 737-based Poseidon in US Navy markings, not a four-engined P-3 Orion.
3. Send it on an ASW patrol. It takes off, flies the patrol and comes back.
4. Open the US Navy purchase list. "P-8A Poseidon (2027)" is listed once. The other P-8 entries are "P-8A Poseidon" (Boeing P-8 Poseidon's `usn_p8`, which has the same name as the retired P-3-bodied copy) and "P-8A" (US Naval Aviation's `usn_p-8a`). Open each of them: every one is a 737-based Poseidon, and none shows a four-engined P-3 body.
5. Run a battle with a P-8 in it, then save, quit to the menu and reload. The P-8s keep their losses and positions.
6. Repeat steps 1 to 4 in the **Regional Expansion**.
7. If you have an old World Sandbox save, open a **copy** of it. Check that it loads and that its P-8s (the P-3-bodied ones it started with) are still there and can take orders.
