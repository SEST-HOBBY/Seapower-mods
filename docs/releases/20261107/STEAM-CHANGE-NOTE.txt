Modernised Worldwide Campaign and UI Feature Update

The original World Sandbox and full Regional Expansion are combined into one Modernised Worldwide Campaign 2028: 156 bases, 293 force groups, 54 patrols and 1,586 catalogue entries. Regional save identity is retained; new content applies to new games.

Added native completion ribbons, artwork and citations for Southern Watch, Southern Reach, Red Line and Sulu Line. Existing third-party awards are preserved.

This Workshop download now includes F11 World Operations (Overview, Forces, Costs and Service), world service progress, campaign cost reporting, and Coordinated Strike styling with planned/remaining weapon-cost estimates. Original strike scheduling and launch controls are retained.

On the supported beta build, run SETUP from the pack folder after downloading. A first installation needs a main-menu check and a second SETUP run. Existing enabled installations update in one run. Rerun SETUP after Steam updates the item. Normal-release active plugins are retained for other builds.
