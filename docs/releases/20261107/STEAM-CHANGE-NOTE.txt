Modernised Worldwide Campaign 2028

The original World Sandbox and complete Regional Expansion are combined into one worldwide campaign entry: 156 bases, 293 force groups, 54 patrols and 1,586 catalogue entries.

The existing regional campaign identity is retained. New content applies to new games; existing saves keep their own state. The original definition and an optional legacy-save selection helper are retained in the pack.

Southern Watch, Southern Reach, Red Line and Sulu Line gain fictional campaign-completion ribbons with artwork and citations in the native Task Force Service Record. Existing third-party ribbon definitions are preserved.
