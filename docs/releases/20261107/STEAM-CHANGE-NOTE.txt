SEST R14 - Sea Power 0.9.2, SEST's code for 0.9.2, Makran Line and Russian Federation

Sea Power 0.9.2: every pack is rebuilt on the game's 0.9.2 files and declares 0.9.2. The game's rebalanced CIWS and its changed combat-system profiles, Western MAD and mini-map settings reach the units that mods' older copies of those files were holding back. The five campaigns write the settings 0.9.0 added to its own campaigns and the unit-acquisition lines 0.9.2 added to Pacific Strike, a British commander shows the game's Royal Navy rank insignia, and the game's new ships, aircraft and rounds, the Hai Shih submarine and the Modern British Navy's Astute and Vanguard classes among them, are priced in the cost list.

SEST's code for Sea Power 0.9.2 Build 261113 (R14: Runtime 1.7.0 / UI 1.5.0, installed by SETUP): World Operations, the F11 cost totals, the Red Line mission ribbons, the runtime corrections and the Contacts, Formation and camera-compass refinements with Pop-Out Windows 1.2.3 are back. A fresh installation needs SETUP, a start to the main menu for about ten seconds, quitting, then SETUP again. On 0.9.2 Anchor Chain's own preloader cannot start Anchor Chain; SEST's loader starts it once Steam has registered the Workshop folders, so with SEST's features turned off, Workshop code mods such as Dynamic Campaign Mod stay off too. Author plugins that call game code 0.9.2 removed, or whose own build check rejects 0.9.2, are held before they load and named in BepInEx\LogOutput.log, with their data kept: Enhanced Decoy Mod, Multi-Stage Missiles, Osprey Mechanics with its deck operations, the flat Topographic Map, and the Alpine runtime fixes, Saronic Corsair strike, MV-75 fixture tilt, EC-2 comms jamming and K5 Kraken contact-strike plugins. They return when their authors update them. Sub Ammunition, Second Thrust, Automatic SAR and Missile Control now load as their authors built them for 0.9.

Red Line mission ribbons: Silent Passage for Under the Convergence's passage made with no weapon fired, and Personnel Recovery for a survivor recovered in Pilot in the Water, are granted at the debrief. As with the game's own ribbons on 0.9.2, a ribbon the commander already holds is not granted again when a mission is replayed.

Makran Line (new): a fifth Task Force campaign of four Pakistan Navy missions in the North Arabian Sea, 24 October to 14 November 2028 - The Fuel Road, Gwadar Roads, Hangor and Port Qasim. Keep the tanker road to Karachi open against the fictional Meridian network's armed merchants and trawlers; no Indian or Iranian state unit is placed. The roster is the Pakistani Pack's: Tughril-class frigates, PNS Alamgir (kept for 2028 as a scenario assumption), the Z-9CE, the pack's Sea King, the JF-17 Block II and III and the ZDK-03. The commander is a Pakistan Navy Commodore; the ribbons are fictional (a completion ribbon, Arabian Sea Security and Trailing Operations). With no Pakistani supply ship in the collection, every force allocation rearms.

Russian Federation: SEST Collection Fixes now registers post-1991 Russian-built ships and submarines (the Gorshkov and Grigorovich frigates, the Steregushchiy, Gremyashchiy and Buyan-M corvettes, the Yasen, Belgorod, Borei and Improved Kilo boats and the Akulas and Oscar IIs launched after 1991), the modern refits of Soviet-built ships in their modern fit only (such as the Kuznetsov's 2016 fit and Marshal Ustinov 2016) and post-1991 Russian aircraft types (Su-57, Su-35S, Su-30SM, Su-33, MiG-31BM, MiG-35 and their like) to the Russian Federation. Cold War types and fits stay Soviet, so stock Cold War missions are unchanged. Only each unit's nation is set; no author's file is replaced.

Missions: when a mission ends in defeat, a protect objective that the same loss failed now shows Failed and costs its points instead of being cancelled. The required-mods lists in each campaign folder now include the mods those mods need.

Collection: the B-21 Raider joins the collection. The standalone J-8, J-11 and Y-8/Y-9 family mods are no longer needed: they were removed from the Workshop, and the PLAAF Aircraft Pack and the PLAN Pack carry those aircraft. SEST now supplies the KQT-8 MAD sensor that the PLAN Pack's Y-8FQ and Y-9FQ mount. Rerun SETUP after this update.
