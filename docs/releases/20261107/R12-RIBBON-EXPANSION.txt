COMMANDER NATIONS
Southern Watch and Southern Reach: Australia (default), US, UK, Japan, each
with the game's rank ladder, name pool, navy name and emblem settings (stock
US and Japan data; a Royal Navy ladder, the stock UK name pool and the White
Ensign from the gallery's public-domain flag set). Red Line: China. Sulu
Line: Philippines. A committed commander keeps its nation; the game refuses
to change it. The same-nation discount is the native rule: it follows the
commander's nation (or the Unrestricted-mode discount-nation setting), so an
Australian commander keeps it on the Australian classes. Each rules page
gives the per-nation class counts.

REAL-AWARD REPRESENTATIONS
Personal and service awards go to a commander of that nation and fit the
campaign's rank (Southern Watch OF-4, Southern Reach OF-5, Sulu Line OF-6).
The host nation's unit award goes to the task group whoever commands it.
  Southern Watch: SW08 Commendation for Distinguished Service / Navy and
    Marine Corps Commendation Medal / OBE (Military); SW12 Distinguished
    Service Cross / Bronze Star Medal / Distinguished Service Order /
    1st Class Commendation; task group Australian Meritorious Unit Citation.
  Southern Reach / Tasman Shield: SR10 AOSM Indo-Pacific / Armed Forces
    Expeditionary Medal; SR12 Commendation for Distinguished Service / Navy
    and Marine Corps Commendation Medal / OBE (Military); TS09 Distinguished
    Service Cross / Legion of Merit / Distinguished Service Order /
    1st Class Commendation; TS12 task group Australian Meritorious Unit
    Citation.
  Sulu Line: SL05 Military Merit Medal; SL07 Distinguished Service Star;
    task group Philippine Republic Presidential Unit Citation.
  Red Line: fictional ribbons only; the PLA designs could not be checked.
Every citation carries its granting mission's date and name and is two
sentences, the second: "Game award for a fictional operation; not a real
honour." The builder stops if a granting mission's title or date drifts,
or if SR10 moves within 30 days of SR01 (the service medals need 30 days).

NATIVE GRANT CONTRACT
Captured SaveDebriefSnapshot checks IsComplete and the mission's
RequiredResult before ApplyMissionRibbonAwards. Awards are pipe-separated,
with comma-separated nation selectors compared with the committed nation,
for example:
  sest_southern_watch_service|australian_distinguished_service_cross,Australia|bronze_star_medal,US
CommanderNations is split on '|', default first. The generator merges
existing definitions and ids idempotently.

WORLD PERSISTENCE AND QUALIFICATION (BETA)
SEST-WORLD-SERVICE-1 and its six fields are unchanged; Runtime 1.6.2 and
1.6.3 read the new record. A task is a standalone order, or a whole
multi-phase operation counted once from DCM's operation result
(OperationId > 0, OrderId 0); phases are not counted. ConvoyRun counts for
Convoy Protection and Route Security, FleetAction for Maritime
Surveillance. Orders and operations are keyed on their id and acceptance
time, so a completion replayed after a reload counts once. Only same-side
Completed results of accepted tasks qualify. Task entries never add to
manual battles, combat USD totals, credits, renown or supplies. A result is
recorded while its paper is unread in the Admiralty queue, so older read
papers are not counted retroactively. Service-record write failures are
logged and retried instead of closing World Operations.

REAL-AWARD CONTEXT AND ART
In-game representations with fictional scenario qualifications, not actual
government awards or claims that SEST operations are declared eligible.
Fictional and most real-award strips are drawn by code from the published
stripe order; no AI imagery, nothing traced. The DSC, Bronze Star,
Commendation Medal and JMSDF cordon use installed Sea Power pictures by
path. AOSM-IP artwork: Nford24, CC0 1.0, copied unchanged (897 bytes),
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Australian_Operational_Service_Medal_-_Indo-Pacific.png
Each campaign's art/ribbons/SOURCES.txt lists its pictures and the award
sources; CREDITS.txt credits the AOSM-IP strip and the White Ensign emblem.
Some ribbon facts came from search extracts because the official sites
could not be reached here; medium-confidence items are the Philippine
colours, the OBE and DSO stripe widths and the Japanese cordon colours.
