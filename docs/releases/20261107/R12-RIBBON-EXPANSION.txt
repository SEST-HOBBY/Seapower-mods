The catalogue retains native award/citation storage and repeated-award counts.
The generator merges existing definitions/IDs idempotently. Real-award images
use installed native asset paths except for the attributed AOSM-IP ribbon.
Old stock citation-sheet images with 1985 text are omitted from these modern
definitions; scenario citations name the SEST Scenario Awards Board.

WORLD PERSISTENCE AND QUALIFICATION
SEST-WORLD-SERVICE-1 and its six fields are unchanged. Accepted completed
orders are added as non-manual entries with typed admiralty keys. Old binaries
ignore them for manual service. Original manual entries and unknown-schema
read-only handling are retained. Deduplication uses the native outcome ID and
timestamp within the existing campaign-definition/side scope. Cross-save
profile scope remains unchanged; no native save is rewritten.

Only same-side Completed outcomes with positive OrderId/outcome Id, a valid
Issued date, At >= Issued, and Moot=false qualify. OrderId=0 operation
summaries do not count a second time. Declined, failed, lapsed, cancelled,
unaccepted and moot outcomes are excluded. Specialist ribbons use CoverConvoy,
Shadow and ClearRoute; variety uses the captured native order-kind enum.
Task entries do not inflate recorded manual games or combat-cost totals.
Ribbons add no economy or promotion rewards. Service refreshes every five
seconds while open; existing direct F11 Combat Costs routing is retained.

REAL-AWARD CONTEXT AND ART
These are in-game representations with fictional scenario qualifications,
not actual government awards or claims that SEST operations are declared
eligible. The DSC represents distinguished command and leadership. AOSM-IP
uses its service-period context: the authored assignment begins 6 December
2028 and SR10 is dated 6 January 2029. The builder rejects this grant if moved
before 30 campaign days. Actual eligibility also requires declared qualifying
duty. US definitions identify personal decorations versus unit citations;
mission completion is explicitly the game's scenario qualification.
