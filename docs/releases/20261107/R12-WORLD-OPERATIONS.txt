Renown and budget rewards remain the native campaign's: battle results and
accepted Admiralty assignments, including escort, shadowing, area control
and multi-phase operations when available. F11 shows actual offered/active
tasks, rewards, failure penalties and offer expiry or deadline. The display
does not pay rewards. Opening it or routine travel cannot grant experience.

Physical supplies remain separate from credits. Production depots feed
convoys, airlifts carry ammunition and loaded replenishment ships support
groups at sea. New fields start at quarter stock, draw from friendly depots
and create no extra income, production, aircraft or ships.

GEOGRAPHY REVIEW
Muharraq base and field: 26.270833 N, 50.633611 E, using the official airport
reference point, with runway 12L true bearing 122.62 degrees. Thirty-six
other airfields were reviewed against public airport reference data.
Longtian and Severomorsk-3 now use their named fields. Both the campaign
marker and the tactical field position are updated. Continuous longitudes
are preserved, including US/Canadian west-coast and Hawaiian locations.

Known limits: a geographic mask does not certify Sea Power terrain. The
existing Fiery Cross and Woody Island models remain explicit small-island
exceptions. Naha and Natuna use retained land-side positions near the
published airport reference points. Eight new fields use generic model
headings where the dataset has no runway direction; ten corrected fields
retain their previous model heading. These are not surveyed airport models.
