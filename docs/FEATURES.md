# SEST Integration Pack — feature list

**SEST Integration Pack - Modernised Campaigns** (Steam Workshop 3812461539) turns
190 Workshop mods for *Sea Power: Naval Combat in the Missile Age* into one
modern game, set in 2028–29. It is one mod folder, sitting at the top of the
Mod Manager, plus a SETUP file that does the ordering. Players subscribe to the
collection *SEST - Modernised Campaign Collection* (3812390790), which brings the
mods the pack needs.

Built for Sea Power 0.8.4. Everything in it is generated by builders in this
repository and checked by automated gates before each upload.

## 1. The campaigns

All four use the game's own **Task Force Mode**, the same system as the stock
Pacific Strike campaign. You buy your force from a priced roster, the story
releases more of it as you go, and losses, damage, ammunition and crew
experience carry forward from mission to mission.

### Southern Watch — The Northern Lifeline
*Royal Australian Navy, October–November 2028*
- 12 core missions keeping the northern sea routes open as a coercion campaign
  turns into a limited regional war: the Arafura, Timor and Banda seas, the
  Biak enclave and the Chinese carrier groups in the Banda approaches.
- 4 optional operations whose results carry into later missions: a classified
  submarine stays classified, Korean warships join if you bring them in, a
  tanker appears if you held the coasters.
- 2 contingency missions.
- 8 single missions in the mission list (*Southern Watch - Dispatches*):
  allied and opposing ships in the same weeks, a weapons range trial, a 2034
  bomber escort and a 1988 exercise that went live.
- Campaign memory: lose HMAS Supply early and her replacement merchant is
  missing later; hold a service window and the next mission is rearmed.

### Southern Reach — Tasman Shield
*Royal Australian Navy, December 2028–March 2029*
- 26 missions in two chapters, three of them optional.
- *Southern Reach*: escort the Antarctic resupply season from Storm Bay across
  the Macquarie Ridge to the ice edge at 60°S.
- *Tasman Shield*: fight the same group from Fiordland through Cook Strait, the
  Tasman, Bass Strait and the Bight to a ceasefire, with New Zealand's
  Poseidons in support.
- 19 campaign variables carry consequences forward: a submarine you sink is
  missing later; an approach you lose or skip reinforces the enemy.
- An optional defector escort (*The Twelve-Mile Line*).
- Every position checked against a real coastline, so ships start afloat in
  narrow water.

### Red Line — The Other Watch
*People's Liberation Army Navy, November 2028–February 2029*
- 6 missions from the other side, as the Chinese carrier group commander the
  Australians only hear on the radio.
- Won by restraint, deception, escort and evasion: classify the submarine
  trailing your carrier without firing on it, get fuel into the Biak enclave,
  stop the one ship in your own company that wants the war back.
- A submarine mission lost the moment the enemy classifies your boat.
- Written to fit the other two campaigns' story whatever happens in it.
- The Open Allocation version sells the PLAN Type 901 and 903A supply ships.
- Its own PLAN navy emblem on the commander screen.

### Sulu Line — The Island Road *(new, October 2026)*
*Philippine Navy, October–November 2028*
- 7 missions in the Sulu Sea and the West Philippine Sea, the same weeks as
  Southern Watch at the other end of the archipelago.
- A Philippine-led task group: Jose Rizal and Miguel Malvar class frigates,
  the Del Pilar class, BRP Conrado Yap, the H-76 helicopter and the Air Force's
  FA-50s. Partners at full price: a Royal Thai Navy frigate with its Seahawk,
  and one RAN Anzac.
- **No free rearm.** After the first mission, ships get ammunition back only
  from a supply ship you bought (BRP Tarlac or Davao del Sur, HTMS Chula, or an
  MSC C8 barge carrier) by going alongside at sea, or from the chartered
  ammunition ship in *Service at Sea*. Supply ships are sold at three points
  only; lose one and its cargo is gone.
- The missions:
  1. *The Island Road*: a convoy down the Palawan coast.
  2. *Fire Mission Jolo*: naval gunfire for the Marines.
  3. *Ayungin*: the shoal resupply run, without firing a shot.
  4. *Service at Sea*: the rearm.
  5. *Celebes Gate*: a gun-runner hunt among real traffic.
  6. *The Aborlan Battery*: two Silkworm launchers that fire back.
  7. *Balabac Strait*: the fleet action against the Chinese southern surface
     group.
- The Philippine flag as navy emblem; Philippine Navy rank ladder.

### In every campaign
- **Open Allocation versions:** the whole roster on sale from the first
  mission. The two Australian campaigns also sell the US, UK, European,
  Japanese and Korean fleets.
- **Fair openings:**
  - no enemy ship or submarine with weapons free starts within 25 NM of you;
  - strike aircraft start at least 20 NM outside their launch range (or at
    least 120 NM out for missiles that reach further than 100 NM).
- **Time you can use:**
  - every mission has a planned time, and reaching it shows a *Behind
    Schedule* message;
  - the mission is lost at one and a half times the plan, and the briefing
    states both times.
- **Weather matters:** ships make less speed in heavy seas, and objectives are
  placed so they can still be reached in time.
- **Briefings:**
  - a photo banner, the theatre, date, side and time window;
  - a briefing chart;
  - photos of your forces and the expected opposition.
- **Campaign art:** a background map for each campaign, a mission card for
  every mission, and printed story pages (newspaper, signal, ship's log,
  intelligence summary).
- **Rules pages:** written for each campaign, with that campaign's discount,
  rearm and availability rules.
- **Same-nation discount:** 20% off units of the commander's own navy; partner
  units cost their listed price, and the rules page says which is which.
- **Checks on every unit:** each unit a mission uses, with its loadout and
  weapon, is checked against the mod that actually wins the load order.

## 2. Replenishment at sea

- **Reloading:** about 2,200 missile and torpedo launchers on modern warships
  had no way to be reloaded (no magazine, no reload flag). They can now be
  refilled from a supply ship alongside, including:
  - deck canisters and fixed tubes;
  - Red Storm Arsenal's per-cell Mk 41 VLS;
  - the Philippine and Thai navies' launchers.
- **Upstream auxiliaries given a tuned supply system:**
  - Sacramento AOE, Kilauea AE, T2 oiler, Boris Chilikin, Kazbek, the Don
    tender, Delvar, Sealift Pacific, Algol and Teide;
  - five merchant ships: the C8 and Seabee barge carriers, Ro-Ro A and B, and
    the Mercur container ship;
  - BRP Tarlac (Philippines) and HTMS Chula (Thailand).
- **Eight modern replenishment ships** as new units, each refitted to its own
  navy and decade:
  - Supply-class T-AOE, Lewis and Clark T-AKE, Henry J. Kaiser T-AO;
  - JMSDF Mashuu, RFA Tide;
  - PLAN Type 901 and Type 903A;
  - Project 23130 Akademik Pashin.
- **HMAS Supply and Stalwart** carry a working supply system.
- **What a supplier can pass is limited:**
  - each has a stock that runs down and is not refilled at sea, a range, a
    speed limit and a size ceiling;
  - heavy strike and area-defence missiles are counted separately, so a small
    oiler cannot hand out Tomahawks.
- **Submarines** can be resupplied too.
- **Stock fixes:** four rounds that had their price and supply category
  stripped by one mod get them back, and broken weapon references (launchers
  that had no round at all) are repaired.

## 5. Cross-mod fixes

When two mods change the same unit, weapon or text, the result is usually
broken. These are the fixes.

**Air-to-ground weapons**
- HARM back to its stock values.
- StormBreaker usable from 500 to 45,000 ft.
- Paveways back to normal behaviour.
- GBU-39 accuracy to its author's 1.5 m.
- KD-88 at its 108 NM range; AKF-98A with its active radar seeker.

**Air-to-air and naval missiles**
- ESSM's anti-jamming bonuses.
- AIM-9L/M and AIM-9X fixes.
- SM-3: a reachable 150,000 ft floor, its loft angle back, and its author's
  seeker and launch profile, with or without the Anchor Chain preloader.
- SM-6 and PAC-3 MSE loft angles back.
- SM-6 and K-SAM II engagement floor down to 15 ft.
- David's Sling ceiling no longer misread as 51 ft.

**Intercept table restored:** one older mod deletes eight of the game's
global intercept values for every mod in the collection. The pack puts the
game's own values back. *Not yet confirmed in game.*

**Gunships, carriers and helicopters**
- AC-130: its 30 mm and its Hellfire launch ceiling.
- Nimitz Expanded: carriers restored, the CVN-70 typo fixed.
- MH-60R: a broken Penguin reference repaired.
- S-70B-2: back under the Australian flag.

**5 October fixes**
- A stale old Spruance (VLS) that showed as "Missing Class" and could throw
  an error on the map, replaced with the current version.
- RAAF F-35A internal-JSM fit no longer shows empty wing pylons.
- Apache AH Mk 1 default loadout no longer draws rocket pods over the
  Brimstones.

**Smaller allied fixes**
- P-8 anti-ship fit pointed at a Harpoon nobody defines.
- HMS Ocean can now operate the Apache.
- RNZAF and ROKN P-8 squadrons named nations with no flag.

**Load order:** every SEST file outranks the mod it replaces, and this is
checked automatically.

## 6. Readability and presentation

- **Encyclopedia photos:** real photographs for 636 units, credited on the
  picture.
- **Offline gallery:** all 517 photos with credits, in the pack folder —
  Open Folder → Gallery → gallery.html.
- **Flags** for 11 nations that showed a missing-flag icon: Peru, Kazakhstan,
  Serbia, Luxembourg, Ireland, Hong Kong, Bahrain, Lebanon, Greenland, French
  Polynesia and Uzbekistan.
- **Names:**
  - 194 unit and squadron names corrected: typos, characters the game's font
    cannot draw, full-width punctuation;
  - Russian names written in Cyrillic now shown in English letters;
  - units that showed as MISSING (Ise, Oberon class, A-4G, S-2 Tracker, the
    Brazilian airbases and others) now have names.
- **English loading tips:** where one mod had replaced them with Chinese,
  and 26 quotations on airpower, deterrence, readiness and logistics behind
  them, each with its speaker and source (`docs/quotes.md`).
- **A quotation under every briefing banner**, chosen by the mission's role
  and different in every mission of a campaign.
- **The Briefing Room:** a mission-browser entry that plays a 73-second film
  of the collection's forces in the right pane, the way the game's own
  Video Tutorials do. Nothing in it is played; it is the pack's title card.
- **Tactical map lines:** black by day, white at night, fully opaque and
  thicker, with the selected route in red.

## 7. Installing and updating

- **SETUP** (`SETUP - double-click me.cmd` in the pack folder), run with the
  game closed:
  - checks every mod in the collection is downloaded and names any that are
    not;
  - installs the Anchor Chain preloader if it is missing, asking Windows for
    permission once if the game folder needs it;
  - turns off a mod's debug logging that can freeze missions;
  - sets the whole Mod Manager order: the pack first, then the mods in the
    tested order, your other mods at the bottom;
  - safe to run again: run it after every pack or mod update.
- **Manual install** for players who would rather not run a script: listed
  step by step in the pinned discussion, with `LOAD-ORDER.txt` and
  `REQUIRED-MODS.txt` in the pack folder.
- **Credits:** `CREDITS.txt` lists every mod file the pack carries a changed
  copy of, and the mod it came from.

## 8. Not yet confirmed in game

These are built and pass every automated check, but have not been watched
working in the game. The play-test guide (`docs/play-test-guide.md`) says
how to check each.

- **Sulu Line's no-rearm rule:**
  - that the game keeps a ship's spent ammunition between missions;
  - that a supply ship's own stock stays used up between missions.
- **The restored intercept table.**
- **Transfers from HMAS Supply and Stalwart.**
- **Red Line's weapons Hold and Tight**, which some missions depend on.
- **The Briefing Room film:** that the mission browser plays a mod's mp4 in
  its right pane as it does the stock tutorials.
