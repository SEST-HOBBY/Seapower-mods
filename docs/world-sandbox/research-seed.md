## Use the research without overstating it

The reports overlap and lack a bibliography sufficient to validate every detailed claim. Repeated statements are not independent corroboration. Keep report-described association, independent verification, fictional scenario allocation, collection mapping and runtime implementation separate.

They establish useful candidate roles and geographic groupings but not a complete current world order of battle, game coordinates, finite stock model or verified 2028 deployment schedule. Leave unsupported counts and locations pending. Proposed activity and population quantities can be authored explicitly without presenting them as facts from the reports.

Priority checks before precise gameplay use include carrier status; resident aircraft/squadrons; DLA scope and centre counts; JLSF organisational terminology; Lingshui assignments; the French facility naming in Djibouti; and the Ban Keun training claim. Avoid deterministic sanctuary, invulnerability, perfect-detection or instant-disable rules taken from rhetorical wording.

Do not include nuclear-storage layouts, material-handling details or nuclear-employment mechanics; they are unnecessary for this populated conventional sandbox.
