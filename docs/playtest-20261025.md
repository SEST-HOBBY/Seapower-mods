# Playtest, 25 October 2026

The owner's notes from a Southern Watch session on the 25 Oct Workshop build
(`b1449201`), and what each became. The session capture
(`chore/capture-playtest-20261025`) had not arrived when the first two fixes
were made; the three items that need its logs or a screenshot wait for it.

| Report | Cause | Change | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| SW02 Steel Highway: the tanker does not follow commands and flies away on its heading | Texaco 51 flew in one formation with Wedgetail 01 (both on the `air` station) and had no waypoints. The tankers seen holding station elsewhere fly alone on a looping track: TS11's Texaco 71 (seen on station) and SW08's Texaco 52. `TaskForceModeIgnoreUnit`, which the tanker carries as a supplied unit, is not the cause: in the 0.8.7 assembly it is read only by the campaign map preview, the Task Force mission generator and the mission editor. | Texaco 51 has its own station behind the convoy and loops a two-point track at 26,000 ft. D2 Flight Deck Day's Texaco 60 had the same arrangement beside Sentry 40 and gets the same fix. The scripts that name either tanker (SW02's "tanker is lost" message, D2's Tanker objective) follow it to its new station. | Fixed; not yet seen in game |
| O2 Southern Cross: the RNZAF P-8 (Kiwi 01) is a very poor model | It was Red Storm Arsenal's `usn_p_8a`, which draws the P-3C Orion's model (`ResourcesRoot=usn_p-3c`). | Kiwi 01 is Euromod RAN's `rnzaf_p8`: a P-8 model, New Zealand's own squadron, ASW fit. It is priced in the cost list. | Fixed; not yet seen in game |
| Debrief: the mission summary's combat cost rows need formatting | SEST appends up to ten `Combat Cost: ...` rows under the native four, two of them sentences (Basis, Incomplete). | Waits for a screenshot of the summary. | Open |

Also from the same day: the World Sandbox flies the same P-3-bodied
`usn_p_8a` for its ten US Navy P-8As (Jacksonville, Bahrain, Djibouti,
Sigonella). Not changed; the owner's call.

SETUP's new compatibility step (`71f20cef`) was run on a stand-in Steam
install with PowerShell 7.4: it marked the 100 out-of-date mods of 199, left
the rest byte-identical, kept every author's file, reported "already marked"
on a second run, and both its own `-RestoreModFiles` and
`mark-mods-compatible.ps1 -Restore` put all 100 back byte for byte.
