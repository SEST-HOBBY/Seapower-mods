# Southern Watch — mod coverage

Generated by `integration/campaign/build_pack.py`. Do not edit.

The campaign is 26 missions built from 382 placed units. Every mod in the canonical load order (`data/load-order.tokens.txt`) and every SEST source pack appears below, with the mechanism that makes the game read its files. Coverage is measured on the load order rather than on catalog status, because five enabled entries are catalogued deprecated and two WIP while still being required donors.

This campaign reaches 207 of the enabled mods and packs; the 4 it does not are listed at the end. The pack's coverage rule - every enabled mod placed or excused - is met by the pack as a whole, not by each campaign in it.

| class | meaning | mods |
|---|---|---|
| `unit` | places the unit; this mod wins its file | 127 |
| `variant` | supplies the hull variant the placed unit uses | 12 |
| `squadron` | supplies the squadron the placed airframe flies from | 6 |
| `store` | supplies a round the placed unit's loadout hangs | 8 |

| `external` | in the collection for a third-party campaign or scenario, or as content none of these campaigns places; required all the same (SETUP checks every mod in the order) | 35 |
| `campaign` | this pack - the campaign being measured | 1 |

Sea and land positions are snapped to points already used by a loading mission; the furthest any anchor had to move is 15.4 NM.
