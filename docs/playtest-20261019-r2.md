  the Arafura Sea, south of Papua and east of the Aru Islands. This point
  is over 36 NM from the committed source coastline. The Growler retains

## Weather Alternate (O4)

The first correction used the narrow passage east of Kai Kecil. It passed
the Natural Earth check but still beached merchants during the user's game
test. That route is removed. No exact cause in the game's terrain or path
following has been established from the report alone.

The fuel ships now start at `-5.96,132.48`, travel north through
`-5.82,132.48`, and stop at `-5.72,132.50`. Their roughly 14.5 NM route stays
over 8.9 NM from the source coastline. The second ship's offset adds about
0.4 NM of travel; both can reach the transfer area inside 70 minutes at
13 knots. The objective is a two-NM arrival area at the final waypoint.

This is an offshore transfer anchorage west of Kai Kecil. Local craft take
the fuel ashore in the mission's fiction; the coasters do not attempt the
inner harbour or island passages. The enemy patrol and fishing boat also
use the western water. All authored O4 sailed legs enforce a four-NM
minimum shore clearance, with the existing 0.3 NM formation spacing retained.

The check samples entire sailed legs, including spawn to first waypoint.
Mission authors may set `route_clearance_nm`; invalid, zero or negative
values fail the build rather than disabling the check. Other missions retain
the existing half-NM default.

## Redback's revised candidate profile

The visible sustainer and its acceleration phase now last ten seconds.
Loft remains approximately 2 km with a 450-knot phase ceiling, followed by a
150-knot cruise target. `Deceleration=0.8` asks the legacy flight model for
a gentler speed transition than its documented default factor of 3.0.
`TerminalVelocity=220` supplies a separate, higher terminal speed target.
The donor rocket, pods, small warhead, 30 NM range, active seeker, target
classes and radio retargeting remain unchanged.

The terminal dive starts at 1.1 horizontal NM. Against a sea-level target,
a 6,562-foot loft over that distance gives an approach geometry of about
44.5 degrees. `IgnoreHeightDifferenceForTargetDist=True` prevents slant range
from shifting that trigger almost overhead. Seeker handoff starts at 2 NM,
before the dive. The global turn-rate cap is reduced from 50 to 20 degrees
per second to soften the transition while retaining target correction.

No fixed-angle terminal-dive field was verified in the exported stock
definitions. The similarly named `TerminalDiveAngle` appears in a few
third-party mods, but that does not establish that the game reads it. It is
not added. This candidate approximates the requested surface-attack shape;
it does not promise an exact pitch, deceleration curve or measured terminal
speed. Target elevation, moving targets, air targets, short shots and launch
height change the geometry. Native guidance remains responsible for the
actual intercept, including air targets where a surface-style dive is wrong.

The stock AS-4 provides `Deceleration`, `TerminalVelocity` and horizontal
target-distance examples. The developer's
[4 December 2024 notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1286220/discussions/0/6330466705287986768)
confirm the legacy deceleration parameter and its former default. These
parameters are used with `ApplyKinematics=False`; this is a fictional game
profile, with a single timed visible motor and no continuous terminal flame.

## Rig Seventeen (SW03)

Three more hostile targets join the existing force:

| Target | Starting station | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Meridian Escort 7, Peykaap missile boat | `-10.30,126.00` | Approaches from the north-west |
| Meridian Raider 2, Boghammar gunboat | `-10.55,126.68` | Approaches from the east |
| Meridian Hind 31, Mi-24P | `-10.05,126.65` | Joins after 15 minutes and flies toward the evacuation/return route |

The original northern missile boat remains. The mission briefing names all
three patrol craft and the later gunship. Difficulty is raised from two to
three. The extra targets stay within the strike role's combat budget; no
destroy-everything objective is introduced. Both lifters must still load
the crew and return, the platform remains protected, and both amphibious
ships must survive. The Apaches already supplied ready on Canberra remain.

   fishing boat as well. Record the map position of any remaining grounding.

   slowdown after the loft, then record dive angle and terminal speed. Test

   Check both 7- and 19-round pods and note short-range behaviour separately.

   cover both lifters through loading and return without damaging the rig.
