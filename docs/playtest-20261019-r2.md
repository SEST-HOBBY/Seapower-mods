## The Open Door (SW08)

- USS Theodore Roosevelt moves from Cenderawasih Bay to `-6.4,135.5` in
  the Arafura Sea, south of Papua and east of the Aru Islands. This point
  is over 36 NM from the committed source coastline. The Growler retains
  a compatible carrier recovery base within the builder's fuel check.
- All seven named Langgur forward strips use the same modern resident wing
  instead of an empty custom group or the stock small-airfield F-4/EA-6 wing.
  The stock scenery, runway and 48-aircraft capacity remain in use. These
  are explicitly authored aircraft from the existing collection, not a
  claim that vanilla's small-airfield default is modern.

recover. Counts, squadron references and deck compatibility are checked

Texaco 52 was gated on `O4LanggurStocked,IsTrue`, including in the standalone
mission where that campaign flag is normally unset. It now always spawns
on a looping 26,000-foot track south-west of the relief box. Completing O4
adds Texaco 53 on a separate track at 28,000 feet. Both have a compatible
recovery field. Briefing, outcome messages and test card agree on that rule.

## Weather Alternate (O4)

The first correction used the narrow passage east of Kai Kecil. It passed
the Natural Earth check but still beached merchants during the user's game
test. That route is removed. No exact cause in the game's terrain or path
following has been established from the report alone.

The fuel ships now start at `-5.96,132.48`, travel north through
`-5.82,132.48`, and stop at `-5.72,132.50`. Their roughly 14.5 NM route stays
over 8.9 NM from the source coastline. The second ship's offset adds about
0.4 NM of travel; both can reach the transfer area inside 70 minutes at
13 knots. The objective is a two-NM arrival area at the final waypoint.

This is an offshore transfer anchorage west of Kai Kecil. Local craft take
the fuel ashore in the mission's fiction; the coasters do not attempt the
inner harbour or island passages. The enemy patrol and fishing boat also
use the western water. All authored O4 sailed legs enforce a four-NM
minimum shore clearance, with the existing 0.3 NM formation spacing retained.

The check samples entire sailed legs, including spawn to first waypoint.
Mission authors may set `route_clearance_nm`; invalid, zero or negative
values fail the build rather than disabling the check. Other missions retain
the existing half-NM default.
