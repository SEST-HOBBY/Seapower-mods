SEST's loader checks DynamicCampaignMod.dll 1.0.2 by SHA-256 (18a2416e...). Only on that exact file and that build, it writes an adapted copy to a private cache on the player's own PC (BepInEx\config\SEST-BetaAnchorChain\...) and loads that copy. Your Workshop file is never touched, and with any other version nothing is adapted. On 261123 the known costs are that contact fading and the scene-editor timer are off, and drawings use the game's new map frame. Source: AssemblyAdapters.Campaign261123.cs.
