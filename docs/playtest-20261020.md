# Fleet and mission playtest update — 20 October 2026

Based on the PC-confirmed private build `d90ac062` (4,084 installed files).
The earlier southern carrier position, western Weather Alternate route,
Texaco availability, Rig Seventeen additions, Seahawk mounts and ten-second
Redback profile remain part of this revision. Start fresh missions and a new
campaign to exercise the changed aircraft, rosters and damage variables.

## Reported aircraft and weapons

### Roosevelt and the Super Hornets

The installed carrier's default wing referred to three incompatible squadron
tables: F/A-18F Squadron3 is fictional ISAF in the winning table; F/A-18E
Squadrons10/19 and EA-18G Squadron11 do not exist there. The default Nimitz
(2025) wing now uses actual US entries without changing its aircraft counts.
Ford's fictional F/A-18F Squadron9 is also changed to a US entry. The changes
are composed in the existing Replenishment builder, which owns those ships.

Two resident Growlers are readied in the conventional SEST SEAD fit. The
airborne SW08 Growler is also US Navy. The carrier stays south of Papua.

All three SEST US Super Hornets (E, F and F Block III) hide the four
`bru-61a` SDB rack meshes in the four-MALICE fit. The three derived AIM-260
fits inherit that correction. Their missiles, tanks and weapon counts are

### Australian Seahawk and Redback

Southern Watch and Southern Reach now use `ran_mh-60r`, Squadron1, with the
Euromod Australian airframe and `ran_mh-60r_816.png` livery. This replaces the
US model with an Australian flag-only squadron in their aircraft, rosters,
flight slots, Langgur residents and RAN ship air groups. Cross-Deck includes
the RAN type. The old US Squadron20 remains for older authored scenarios.

The Redback active-seeker conversion had retained a laser donor without an
active radar transmitter. It now declares Ka-band and the Hellfire donor's
11 kW transmitter, an explicit 0–20,000 ft target band, and surface attack as
its primary AI role. Air targets remain a secondary class; the unused passive
seeker range is zero. The same small warhead and missile/pod meshes remain.
This corrects incomplete configuration; it does **not** establish that the
reported helicopter cancellation was caused solely by those omissions.

The ten-second motor, 150-knot cruise target, approximately 45-degree
surface-approach geometry, 220-knot terminal speed target, moving-target
memory and radio retargeting remain. Those are game tuning values, not
measured flight performance. Try the actual RAN model with Redback against a
moving destroyer before relying on it in a campaign.

*Superseded on 23 Oct 2026* ([the Redback follow-up](playtest-20261023-redback.md)).
In the game the RAN MH-60R listed the AGR-30 against a boat and never fired
it, and its pod sat at the bottom of the quad Hellfire launcher. On the
evidence, the radio command needed a guidance channel the Seahawk's
hardpoint does not link, so the round is fire and forget again. The owner
saw it fire from the next build (`57375598`). After the owner's second report that
day the MH-60R's pods went onto its Hellfire launcher in the Hellfires'
place, the tanks kept, and the flight was set from the game's own rules: a
motor that works when an aircraft fires it, a hard run-up, the 2 km climb
on short shots again, and a steady slow-down to the 150-knot cruise. Those
changes are not yet seen in game.

### ARRW

The two Dingtools AGM-183 variants had a 20-second straight-flight phase;
the testbed `usn_arrw` had 35 seconds. Both could burn visibly while flying
flat. All three now have a 0.5-second initial guidance phase and 1.5-second
release drop, followed by the existing steep loft request.

All use one 35-second timed motor, with no continuous in-flight flame. Mesh
separation is aligned at 35 seconds. Dingtools' main `AGM` mesh was not the
ARRW author's separate glide vehicle: all three now use the author's model
block, `arrw.obj`, launch mesh `agm-183`, main mesh `Glider`. The conventional
and W62 stats otherwise remain their own. The OBJ bytes are Workshop assets,
absent from this text-only export: actual mesh resolution, carriage position,
boost trajectory and gliding presentation require the PC test.

an individual tactical report at minute eight. The reports come from the
command team and contain decisions and story detail, not magical detections.
Ellis's observation desk, the red-spider marking, Ward, Prasetyo, Kila, Li,

engagement. New reports use native time/intelligence triggers.

The Drone Yard keeps its original strike plan: Mapun has no safe friendly
recovery location for this short-range detachment. No extra airfield is
placed on the hostile island and no endurance is invented to cover the gap.

Independent submarines are added to Fujian's Shadow, Broken Supply Line,
Cold Route, Tasman Crossing, The Southern Convoy, Approaches and Balabac
Strait. They are controllable operation-specific attachments, submerged with
weapons held, not unsupported purchases. Losing Rankin in Broken Supply Line
removes her from Cold Route. The existing authored submarine missions remain.

## AIM-424/AIM-260 range investigation

No range buff is applied without an observed cause. These are the winning
configuration values, not real-world specifications or guaranteed launch
solutions:

| Round | Declared launch cap (NM) | Active seeker (NM) | Passive seeker (NM) | Passive mode |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| SEST AIM-424 | 290 | 40 | 80 | Full radar/emitter homing |
| Dingtools AIM-260 | 125 | 32 | 32 | Home-on-jam |
| Winning AIM-174B | 325 | 15 | 15 | Home-on-jam |

MALICE/JATM/Gunslinger have different seeker gain, transmitter power, burn,
drag and loft settings. Their configured `AntiJammerBonus` values are
0.95/0.75/0.98 respectively; Gunslinger also has higher seeker gain
(65 dB versus 62/60). Its Anchor Chain model overwrite changes the model and
motor presentation, not a separate launch-range or guidance cap.
Small-RCS fighters, changing aspect, target speed,
track quality and jamming can change an offered solution independently of
the file's launch cap. MALICE's full passive mode is especially relevant to
the reported AEW/emitter distinction. This is an inference from the files,
not a measured diagnosis of the PC engagement.

The developer's [0.8.3 update notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1286220/allnews/)
describe changes to range-dependent jamming, radar burn-through, noise jamming,
missile resistance and datalink/OODA behaviour. They support investigating
the sensor/engagement state; they do not establish a specific cause here.

Use D10/D11 to record weapon and target, displayed/actual launch range,
shooter speed/altitude, target altitude/aspect, radar/ECM state, track
classification, midcourse updates, seeker activation and outcome. Formation
spacing means the three shooters have small lateral offsets; record actual
range and use the same shooter/target in the matched Clear/Contested runs.
The pairs are controlled starting conditions, not deterministic tests.

## Proposed optional plugins

[Formation Manager Replacement](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3818251045)
and [Combat Cost Tracker](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807187341)
are not added to the required items or canonical order in this private build.
Neither plugin's files are present in the captured install. Their descriptions
require Anchor Chain, which the current collection already uses. The first
item's description was supplied by the player; its page could not be retrieved
during this review. No binary compatibility claim is made for either plugin.

Formation Manager's compact tiles and aircraft grouping suit the expanded
forces. Trial it first on a fresh mission with BetterTacMap: check selection,
filters, hover highlights and grouping at different zoom levels, then reload
the save and restart the game to check settings persistence. The captured
BetterTacMap version adds map controls, so the shared map behaviour needs an
actual game check even though the proposed manager retains native commands.

Combat Cost Tracker is a useful second trial. Its author documents F11,
restart after enabling, and a fresh mission for complete loss accounting;
AC Pack is not required. Its estimate overlay is separate from the native
campaign purchase budget. Unknown SEST equipment prices should remain unknown
until the plugin catalogs and exact SEST IDs are checked. Central catalog
entries, including blanks, override inline costs, so adding speculative values
to weapon INIs would not be a reliable integration.

The author's current comment thread contains an unresolved player report of
null-reference errors on 18 October with original Anchor Chain. This is not a
confirmed SEST conflict. Enable the tracker separately after the manager trial
and record its log if it reproduces. Neither optional plugin is included in the
this build's static validation.
