## PC checks for the builder grammar (10 November 2026)

The build steps of 10 Nov 2026 (build notes, "10 November 2026: alternative victories, the heave-to and progress reports") use only stock trigger pieces, but three combinations the Red Line build writes have no exact stock line: two area tests in one trigger (RL04's challenges, RL03's Disengage), AND NOT on a loss (RL03's Disengage and RL04's HoveTo path), and two NOTs in one expression (RL03's Disengage). Stock negates only an area test or a classification, once per expression; P2, P3 and P9 cover the negated loss. A fourth, telegraph 0 with no waypoint, waits behind the OFF damage-state flag (P1). These behaviours need a game:

- **P1 UnitDamageState spelling.** In the Mission Editor, save one trigger with *Unit damage state*, mode *All propulsion systems inoperable*, and a second with *Integrity below threshold* at 50%. Send the `Condition_ConditionN_*` keys and values the editor writes. They fill `campaign_data.DAMAGE_STATE`. Until then the flag stays off and RL04 offers no "stop her engines" outcome.
- **P2 RL03 Disengage.** With an F-35 inside 70 NM of the return box's centre, the mission must not end. With neither shot down, once both F-35s are beyond 70 NM of it, or have gone home after spending their missiles, it must end within about 5 seconds, and never before minute 18. The debrief shows "the F-35s have broken off" and Fighters is not completed. This confirms that North Borneo's refresh shape (`Taskforce=Taskforce2`, `AND NOT`, a 5-second reactivate) behaves as in stock.
- **P3 TriggerCompleted on a late trigger.** RL04's *Turns back: HeaveTo* ships disabled and is enabled by a challenge. Its having fired must satisfy the HoveTo path: once she has hove to and both ships are in the withdrawal circle with nothing inbound, the mission is won with the "has hove to" text.
- **P4 RL04 heave-to.** With the lead ship at Hold, close Meridian Harmony. Within about three miles she must change to a neutral symbol, take the new waypoint north-west and slow to telegraph 2. The ship's flight alone must not do it. Note the range when it happens. A frigate at Tight must stop engaging her once she is neutral. Sinking her afterwards must end the mission with "had hove to and was following the group when she was sunk". The *Progress: Running* report ("halfway to clearing the screen") must not appear after she has turned. In a second run, sink her from beyond three miles, then sail the lead ship through the water she passed: no heave-to line may come, and the mission must still be won once the group is in the withdrawal circle.
- **P5 Hold clock.** Item 7 above. "Pilot aboard." must come four minutes after the lead ship is within three cables, not on arrival. This also covers Southern Watch SW03's three-minute hold.
- **P6 RL03 attack orders out of range.** The F-35s' `Attack=` orders fire at 600 s and 960 s at Dragon Eye. If the KJ-500 is outside AIM-120 range, does the F-35 close and fire, fire anyway, or drop the order? This decides how often Disengage opens without a fight.
- **P7 Meridian Harmony's speed.** At telegraph 4 in sea state 2, measure her knots and the minute she reaches the marked circle south-east. The challenge chain runs along her route to that circle, so her speed decides whether the lead ship can catch her on it.
- **P8 Main flags.** In RL01 (Raiders), RL04 (Spoiler) and RL06 (Qship), the second Main objective must only change the objectives list and the debrief. It must not change the win or the loss, and a win must not end with that objective failed.
- **P9 RL03 in one tick.** Shoot down one F-35 before minute 18, then bring Dragon Eye into the box after minute 18 with the other F-35 beyond 70 NM. Expect one mission end and one victory message, the Intercept path's ("the intercept is broken"), and no "Progress" line with it.

The design's P10 (an RLC1 return leg) is left out: RLC1 has no return leg, by the owner's choice.

Progress reports (RL02, RL03, RL04 and RL06) are intel lines only, named *Progress: ...*. Note any that arrives at a moment it misstates.

## PC checks for the runtime ribbons (granted by the beta runtime R13 only, 11 November 2026)

Two fictional ribbons are granted by SEST's beta runtime R13 (Runtime 1.6.5), not by the game: **Silent Passage** (`sest_silent_passage`, RL05, `[Mission15]`) and **Personnel Recovery** (`sest_personnel_recovery`, RLC1, `[Mission11]`). The campaign names them in `SestRuntimeRibbonAwards`, a key the game ignores, and defines them in `commander_settings.ini`, outside `RibbonIds`. R12 (Runtime 1.6.4) ignores the key too; this build's beta runtime is R13, which SETUP installs on the beta. R5 and R6 can be run now, on this build: the developer ribbon menu is the game's own code and reads only `RibbonIds`. R1 to R4 need R13. Under R13 each debrief logs two Player.log lines for each runtime ribbon, `[SEST Runtime] debrief mission N: runtime ribbon <id> ...`, each with the result, objectives, launch and round counts and survivors rescued it was judged on. Judge each check by the first line, the verdict. The second line is expected: after an award it says "already awarded"; otherwise it repeats the first line's "not met: ...". It comes because Red Line's `campaign.ini` names itself as its `[File] Base`: the game's `EnumerateCampaignRuleSources` yields the file once as the open campaign ini and again as its base, so R13 reads the key twice. A second AWARDED for the same ribbon, or a third line, is a fault: report it. If a later R13 skips a value it has already read, expect one line.

- **R1 Silent Passage.** Complete RL05 at Costly Victory or better without launching anything. The first `sest_silent_passage` line says AWARDED with launched=0 and rounds=0, and its objectives show Passage completed; the second says "already awarded". The debrief's awards row shows Undersea Operations and Silent Passage; the Service Record shows Silent Passage with its citation, dated 27 December 2028, and the rank held.
- **R2 Not silent.** Repeat RL05 with one torpedo (a launch that fails counts too): the first line says "not met: nolaunch" and the second repeats it, and Undersea Operations is still awarded. Repeat using only noisemakers or decoys: still eligible; note whether they appear in the first line's "all launched" count. Launch a weapon, save, reload and finish without another: still not eligible.
- **R3 Personnel Recovery.** Win RLC1. The first `sest_personnel_recovery` line gives rescued=N, which must equal the debrief's survivors rescued. Note whether the four-minute hold alongside the raft raises N, whether lifting the pilot with the ship's helicopter does, and when the count rises (pickup or landing). If N stays 0 after a win, the first line says "not met: rescued>=1 (rescued 0)" and the second repeats it: the game does not count the authored raft, and the award needs another condition before R13 ships. Report it.
- **R4 Once only.** After an award, save, quit, reload and open the Service Record: each ribbon shows once. Replay the mission: the Service Record still shows it once. A failed attempt (below Costly Victory) grants neither ribbon.
- **R5 Not in the developer list.** Now, on this build's R13, and on R12: the developer ribbon menu does not offer Silent Passage or Personnel Recovery, and its grant does not hand them out.
- **R6 R12, the standard build or feature off.** On R12 (Runtime 1.6.4), on the standard build, or under R13 with `MissionAwards=0` under `[Runtime]` in `plugins/sest_runtime.ini`: neither ribbon is granted, no runtime ribbon line is logged, and RL05 still grants Undersea Operations.

Old restraint-only timings, GOLF's automatic weapons-free search and thirty-minute decoy guidance have been superseded. The campaign bible's dated revision describes current goals. Return mission, step, time, outcome and Player.log for any failure.
