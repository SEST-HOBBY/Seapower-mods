MV-75 Cheyenne II

Select Bell Systems Test in the mission menu for eight flight and systems trials.
Navy and Army liveries include green flight uniforms, black goggles and occupied cockpits.
The aircraft has a 280-knot cruise target, 305-knot maximum speed, 2,100-nm ferry range and a 20,000-ft ceiling. Loaded range varies with carried stores and flight profile.

01 Torpedo and Sonobuoys: two Mk46 torpedoes, dipping sonar and twelve buoys.
02 Hellfire Surface Strike: eight missiles and a surface target.
03 Door Miniguns: targets on both sides.
04 Radar FLIR and Warning Sensors: surface and submarine contacts.
05 Countermeasure Threat: a hostile missile ship.
06 Night Carrier Lighting: launch, recovery, navigation and landing lights.
07 Land Rearm and Loadouts: land operations, loadout changes and a second sortie.
08 Army Land Launch: Army aircraft at an airfield, with a stock Sea King for comparison.

ASW carries torpedoes and twelve sonobuoys in two wing-mounted cages. Strike carries Hellfires. Transport keeps eight passenger places.
The occupied interior includes two pilots, two door gunners, eight seats and green instruments.
Start the torpedo trial at 500 ft, inside the stock 100 to 1,000 ft release envelope.
Dipping sonar requires a stationary low hover; retract it before climbing to release.
Use mission 07 to check landing gear, recovery, rearming and a second launch.
Use mission 06 to check cabin lighting, EMCON and save/reload.
Use mission 08 for Army airfield operations. Rotor movement begins with engine startup and reaches full speed in about 12 seconds.
The two miniguns use rotary barrel motion, muzzle flash and looping firing audio.

Requires the existing compatible Anchor Chain and Harmony loading arrangement.
Disable MV-75 Cheyenne II in the mod list to remove these aircraft and trials from the active session.
