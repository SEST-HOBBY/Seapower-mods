Anduril Maritime Drones

Enable Anduril Maritime Drones in the mod menu and restart Sea Power. No other mods are required.

Included vehicles
Dive-LD: survey, mine countermeasures and a variant with two Copperhead-100M torpedoes.
Dive-XL: variants with four Copperhead-500M or 24 Copperhead-100M torpedoes.
Copperhead-100 and Copperhead-500: unarmed survey vehicles.
K5 KRAKEN: reconnaissance, a strike variant with two Copperhead-100M torpedoes, and one-way attack.

Scenarios
Find all four missions under Anduril Maritime Trials: Dive-LD Mine Clearance, Copperhead Strike, K5 KRAKEN One-Way Attack and Anduril Showcase. The showcase brings all ten variants onto one daylight range.

Controls and tips
Give survey vehicles waypoints. Without a route they remain near their starting position.
Steer the Dive-LD MCM through the mine lane at low speed.
Fire Copperheads ahead of a moving target. Fast ships can outrun a torpedo fired from behind.
For the K5 one-way attack, steer a parallel course alongside the target and release the strike payload within 35 m. The blast destroys the boat.
Undersea vehicles cannot recharge at sea. Armed variants carry no reloads.
The Mission Editor includes years through 2031. These units enter service in the editor in 2026.

Stores
The Copperhead-100M is available for US Mk 32 tubes and the Saronic Corsair torpedo cradle when that mod is enabled. The Copperhead-500M is available for US 533 mm submarine tubes. Select the torpedoes in the Mission Editor stores list.
The MV-75 Cheyenne II mod provides its own Copperhead loadout for its Army and Navy variants.
