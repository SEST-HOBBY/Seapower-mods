Define SubmarineRegainSonarContact

If ContactLevel < 5

	SetCourse ($BearingToContact)

	SetDepth ($BelowLayer)
	
	Begin Timer
	
	If ContactLevel > 5
	
		Begin SubmarineTrackContact
	
	Else
	
		When Timer = 2
	
		SetTelegraph 1
		
	Begin Timer
	
		If ContactLevel > 5
	
		Begin SubmarineTrackContact
	
	Else
	
		When Timer = 2
		
			SetTelegraph 3
		
			SetDepth ($AboveLayer)
		
			SetTelegraph 1
		
		Begin Timer
		
			If ContactLevel > 5
	
			Begin SubmarineTrackContact
	
		Else
		
			When Timer = 2
			
			Begin SubmarineSprintAndDrift
