gente_victory_photo.png and gente_victory_finish.png (victory magazine)
Reused from the previously reviewed editable GENTE cover, based on the supplied 6 May 1982 soldiers cover. The photograph was AI-restored in that earlier artwork workflow and contains reconstructed detail. It is not an unaltered archival photograph. The black headline panel conceals the lower reconstructed area.
END_V_gente_en.xml and END_V_gente_es.xml adapt that design to the fictional campaign victory on 29 May 1982. The WE HAVE WON / HEMOS GANADO headline is campaign-authored, not a historical GENTE headline from that issue. Original photographer and publication rights have not been established by this adaptation.

19820516_intel_event.xml and 19820516_intel_event_es.xml (Message 090)
Source: Ministerio de Defensa, AR_EA_CEM_CEM_04_02, PDF page 181, printed page 93-121.
https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/ar_ea_cem_cem_04_02.pdf
Military Governor's Message No. 090 is reproduced under 151130May82 in the Army's later Comision Especial Malvinas chronology. The XAML is an adapted single-entry extract, not a facsimile of the original transmitted signal. The retrospective commission heading, unrelated chronology entries and handwritten archival annotations are omitted. The two-column date/time and events structure is retained.
Spanish spelling and punctuation are lightly regularized. SKYVAN corrects the printed EKYVAN. The unexplained N ECT following rocket-launcher tubes is omitted. English is a translation of that edited Spanish text. Reported aircraft losses and uncertainty are retained as contemporaneous claims, not retrospectively corrected totals. No timezone has been added.
The existing UK moon_paper_wear texture is reused at low opacity. Text remains editable XAML. The legacy May 16 filename is retained to preserve its existing reference, while the card identifies the message date as May 15.
