NEGOTIATED ENDING NEWSPAPER

Visual references
User-supplied Cape Cod Times photographs, Saturday 18 July 1981 (Georges Bank)
and Saturday 14 August 1982 (offshore gas). The second supplies the alternate
teal edition-strip panels, gull masthead, greeting and narrow edition line.
The fictional newspaper is Harwich Times. Its printing office is
91 Georgia Lane, Harwich, MA, an alternate-history location supplied by the user.
The masthead asset harwich_times_masthead.png is an imagegen reconstruction
of the supplied design with Cape Cod replaced by Harwich. It is not a
pixel-identical extraction. The user prioritized the masthead appearance,
so it is a separate raster asset shared unchanged by both language editions.
Other headings, metadata, captions and story text remain editable XAML.
The historical scans are supplied references. No public-domain claim is made
for their original masthead artwork. Historical articles are not reproduced.

Campaign adaptation
END_N_newspaper_en.xml and END_N_newspaper_es.xml contain original fictional
reporting dated 31 May 1982. Successful mediation by Reagan and Haig is the
campaign's alternate-history outcome, not a claim about the real war.
The existing ending terms are retained: phased British and Argentine withdrawal,
international supervision, interim UN administration and unresolved sovereignty.
The Spanish file translates the American edition. English is its original language.

Historical mediation context
US Department of State, Foreign Relations of the United States, 1981-1988,
Volume XIII, document 96, Haig to Reagan, 12 April 1982.
https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1981-88v13/d96
This documents the historical mediation and interim-administration questions.
It is not evidence that the fictional May agreement occurred.

Dependencies and validation
Arial and Times New Roman are Windows font dependencies, as in adjacent papers.
Images: harwich_times_masthead.png, ar_reagan_haig_cabinet_1981.jpg and the
campaign's existing moon_paper_wear.png. Five visual refinement passes in both
languages, with WPF previews using the NoesisWindows profile. Story paragraphs
were checked against the preceding negotiated edition. English is the original
language and the Spanish copy is the translated American edition.
Unity/Noesis appearance and language switching remain untested in game.
