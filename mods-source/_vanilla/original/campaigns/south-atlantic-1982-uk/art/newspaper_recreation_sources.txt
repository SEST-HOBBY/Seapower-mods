EMPIRE COVER
Reference: user-supplied 19820426_newspaper_original_playtest_empire_strikes_back.png.
hermes_clean_background.png was made with the built-in image editing tool, using that cover as the edit target. The edit removed printed text, price, registration mark and barcode and reconstructed the image behind them. The original photograph's composition was retained as closely as the tool allowed; this is an AI-edited derivative, not a pixel-identical recovered photograph. It has not been halftoned. The underlying photograph's rights were not independently established.
See HERMES-EDIT-BRIEF.txt for the editing instructions.
All main text, secondary text and lower-left caption are overlaid as editable XAML. The only replacement title is the paper name, supplied in two versions. The barcode and registration mark were omitted.
Printed date retained from the reference: April 19, 1982. The campaign filename date is April 26.
The other two supplied Hermes photographs were supporting references and are not bundled or used in the final artwork.

HALFTONE METHOD
Both portraits use a 450 x 470 logical drawing, rendered at 4x resolution. Dot pitch is 2.7 logical pixels on a diagonal square grid. Dot radius varies with grayscale luminance. The dots are in separate PNG images, not thousands of XAML shapes.
