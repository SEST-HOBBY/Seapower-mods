ARGENTINA CAMPAIGN SINGLE BRANCH NOTES

- Argentina campaign is deliberately ahistorical as the player will play the role of Anaya/Lombardo in controlling their forces
- Argentina campaign roughly runs late April to May 21 (continuing into June doesn't make sense in this context)
- Objectives for Argentina would be to find and sink the British carriers, OR destroy enough amphibious lift to make the invasion too politically costly to proceed
- Through line here is that the Argentine player should receive intel expecting the British to land at Stanley (as in real life the Argentines thought the Brits would do a USMC style forced landing), and San Carlos should be somewhat "unexpected" in terms of player force composition and location
- Overall rule for proper missions: missions that ONLY feature aircraft should be avoided - actions should center around ships or submarines, and where control of ships is not really possible (such as the bombing raids against San Carlos) should feature many aircraft that must be managed. Still these types of missions should be avoided if possible - they do not really work in the scale of SP.
- We need a mission that allows limited A-4s to base at Stanley. Perhaps a convoy protection mission that brings the supplies needed to re-grade the airstrip and brings in the supplies to operate A-4s and Canberras

TIMELINE

AR00 Operation Rosario - 2 April
- Command Santa Fe to deliver divers, control Santisima Trinidad to cover landing ships into Port William - as the only opposition would be land-based, perhaps this mission is better done as a prologue slide (or set of slides)

AR01 Grytviken Convoy - 25 April
- Escort Bahia Buen Suceso and Rio Carcaranas to Grytviken to secure South Georgia with a larger presence - player fights off the South Georgia task force (Antrim, Plymouth, Brilliant, Tidespring). Player assets: Hercules, Py, Drummond or similar + helos, 707 MPA

AR02 First Blows - 1 May
- Defend Stanley against the encroaching British bombardment group; sink one bombardment group ship to force the others to retreat. Win by keeping part of the shore installations intact and driving off the bombardment group. This will rely on despawn zones for the Mirage/Dagger/A-4 flights which cannot land at Stanley; player will have the shore batteries, Roland, Pucaras at the airfield. Super Etendards could be introduced here if the player wants to expend some of their missiles (TBD)

AR02A San Luis Patrol - 2 May
- Control ARA San Luis to attack a British escort group of 2 ships north of the islands - sink one escort to win and withdraw, sink both to win outright. If San Luis is lost here then it should remain lost for the entire campaign - we need a method to force players to win OR lose scenarios without allowing total restarts?

AR03 Carriers at War - 2 May
- Reverse of the UK03 scenario, not much to it - objective is to sink or damage Hermes or Invincible (as we do not yet have Hermes, use Illustrious as standin). Perhaps a time limit can be placed on this mission to allow one strike by the player's A-4Q, Belgrano will not be involved here (it will come later). Sinking a carrier here will not end the campaign just remove it from later missions

AR03A Breaking Contact - 2 May
- Reverse of the HMS Conqueror/Belgrano mission - bring Belgrano west to Staten Island/Ushuaia without being sunk by the submarine. We need better submarine AI for this to work (!) - if this mission is skipped, Belgrano is lost assumed sunk by the HMS Conqueror - if this mission is won, unlocks AR04. State prominently: "Warning: if you advance without completing this mission, Belgrano may be lost."

AR04 Attack on the Screen - 4 May
- Exocet day against Sheffield - With the carrier requiring replenishment after AR03, the airforce is used to strike the remaining battlegroup SE of the islands - objective is to sink an escort, but the player can always try to hit other groups (RFAs or even carriers). If Belgrano survived, its group can be used here

AR05 Night in the Sound - 10 May
- 2 ship maximum, stop a surface sweep through the Falkland Sound - compact night action, 2 ship max - win by sinking the British frigate attempting to force the sound, neutral contacts are the Argentine blockade runners trying to unload and must not be targeted

AR05A Hunter off the Coast - 11 May
- DeMayo has moved back towards the coast but is being tracked by at least 2 submarines. Use Trackers, Sea Kings, and the destroyers to locate and sink both. Success removes the enemy submarines; failure loses the carrier; skipping has no effect.

AR06 Stanley Convoy - 12 May
- Escort a freighter carrying runway building supplies to the islands - fight through a British interception force (get the convoy to a trigger zone) - individual ships must be tracked, one carries A-4 spares, one carries runway supplies, one fuel etc - losing them deducts capability from the followign missions

AR07 Battle off Stanley - 15 May
- Fight off a concerted attack by the enemy carrier(s) against Stanley and Goose Green - use Pebble Island airstrip with Pucaras and mainland bomb attacks, OR the improved runway at Stanley to destroy two British bombardment groups. Use a small surface force here, Belgrano, etc depending on what is alive.

AR07A RFA Raid - 16 May
- Command either ARA San Luis or ARA Santa Fe to ambush a tanker pumpover rendezvous - needs a victory consequence beyond just blowing things up

AR07B Fuel for the Fleet - 17 May
- Defend the Argentine tanker and receiving ships from a British submarine; success removes the submarine and should affect the available surface forces in the next main missions (as their fuel is lost)

AR07C The Outer Picket Station - 19 May 0300L
- Requirement: 2 x Drummond class corvette, Argentine accounts mention these small ships were hard to find on radar - Corvettes close to destroy the a picket destroyer and then withdraw before first light (TBD if we keep this one)

AR08 Attack on the Task Force - 19 May
- Decisive coordinated strike to remove the carrier(s) and their threat - should use almost all of the player's available strike assets, ships and aircraft, Belgrano group. If neither carrier has already been sunk, sinking one is the main objective. If AR03 achieved it, this becomes an operation to sink the remaining carrier OR drive it away (need to understand how we do this)

AR09 Amphibious Approach - 20 May

Find and attack the remaining invasion convoy before it can enter Falkland Sound. AR10 Settlement at San Carlos - 21 May
