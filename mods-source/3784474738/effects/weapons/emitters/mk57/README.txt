Mk57 twin vent effects
The normal and Quad vent fire is sampled on both mesh strips in ContainerBase
space. The flame root also owns the ascending World-space smoke. Normal adds
a warm cap. Quad has the lower neutral plume. Birth offsets are zero and the
two ports share one emission budget and one shadowless point light.
The separate, delayed firing-cell effect contains filled radial deck smoke.
