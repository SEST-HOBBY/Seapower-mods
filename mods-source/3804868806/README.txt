Anduril FQ-44 Carrier Wingmen

Six fictional naval variants: Escort, Interceptor, Strike, SEAD, Recon and EW.
The recon aircraft carries no weapons or pylons and has the lowest radar signature.
The armed variants have higher signatures; these remain fixed after weapon release.
Radar and ESM use native datalink. EW uses directional jamming; aim the jammer to cover the supported flight.
Place a crewed fighter first in a Vic formation, followed by FQ-44 wingmen. Match its speed to the FQ-44 flight.
Gray US Navy paint with subdued national insignia and aircraft numbers. Paired underwing racks carry four weapons across the armed variants. Carrier adaptation, flight performance and sensor strengths are fictional.
