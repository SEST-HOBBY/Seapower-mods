Drone Control

Supports Valkyrie and Fury Escort, Interceptor, Strike, SEAD, Recon and EW. Requires Alpine Mod Loader.

Start a mission with a supported player aircraft airborne. Right click the drone and choose Drone Control. Its feed opens at the side of the main 3D gameplay view. Flight orders continue while you use the camera.

Point at the feed and use WASD or arrow keys to look around. Drag across the image to pan with the mouse. Manual input stops automatic tracking. Resume tracking follows the assigned contact again. Outside the feed, the normal gameplay controls remain available.

Select a current surface contact. Watch aims the sensor without changing flight orders. Shadow orders an orbit around the contact. Detach from a formation before using Shadow. Watch also works in formation.

DAY, NIGHT, WHITE HOT and BLACK HOT select the image mode. Wide, Medium and Narrow select zoom. One feed is open at a time. Choosing another aircraft switches the open feed to that drone. Close feed releases the camera and leaves flight orders intact.

Orbit settings controls distance, altitude, direction and the response to losing contact. SAM standoff is on by default. Disabling it accepts the risk shown by the panel. Resume route ends the orbit and retains the sensor assignment. Return home uses the native return order. Fuel and emergency behavior take priority.

Drag the title bar to move the window and its bottom right corner to resize it. Click the title or arrow to collapse it. The X closes the feed and hides the window. Choose Drone Control from an aircraft menu to reopen it. The window remembers its position, size and collapsed state.

Shadow orders and assigned contacts survive mission saves. After loading, reopen the feed. Image modes and zoom are session controls.
