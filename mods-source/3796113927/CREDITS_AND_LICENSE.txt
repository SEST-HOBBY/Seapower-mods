This package
------------
The Sea Power OBJ conversion, object split, animation INI, aircraft INI,
localization, and packaging in this release are provided for non-commercial
modding and testing. No source ordnance meshes or ordnance models are
redistributed here. Existing air-to-air, anti-ship, and strike ammunition
continues to be referenced from the external Dingtools Weapon Pack. The only
local weapon definition is the user-supplied `usn_mk50_air.ini`; it references
the game's built-in MK-46 model and material and does not add a weapon asset.

Technical reference notes

Public-source links used for the non-authoritative equipment and airframe
