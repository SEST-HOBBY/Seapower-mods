Hyūga Class — Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force helicopter destroyers. Hyūga (DDH-181) entered service in 2009; Ise (DDH-182) followed in 2011. The class supports helicopter operations, anti-submarine warfare, command duties and disaster relief.

PUBLISHED CLASS SPECIFICATIONS: Standard displacement 13,950 tonnes; length 197 m; beam 33 m; draft 7 m. Four gas turbines, two shafts, 100,000 PS and 30 knots. Published class complement: approximately 380 personnel. Standard displacement is not full-load displacement.

REAL-SHIP ARMAMENT: The JMSDF lists two 20 mm close-in weapon systems, a vertical-launch system, two torpedo-launcher installations and patrol helicopters. Actual missile and aircraft loads can vary; the quantities below describe this mod.

OUR SHIP — WEAPONS: 16 VLS cells: four quad-packed ESSM cells (16 missiles) and twelve VL-ASROC cells (12 missiles). Two Phalanx CIWS and two triple 324 mm torpedo launchers; the torpedo magazine is configured for 12 Mk 54 rounds. These ammunition choices are the mod loadout, not a verified real-ship load.

OUR SHIP — AVIATION: Both named variants default to four SH-60J and four SH-60K helicopters. The mod sets capacity to 18 aircraft, with two elevators, four launch points and two recovery points. Scenario aircraft assignments may differ; these are game settings, not real operating limits.

OUR SHIP — SENSORS: FCS-3, OPS-20 and OQQ-21 are represented by gameplay profiles based on the Euromod Asahi reference and stock sensors. Configured ranges: search radar 347 km; surface/navigation radar 46.3 km; hull sonar 60 km active and 70 km passive. These are simulation parameters, not verified real equipment ranges or guaranteed detection distances. Electronic support and countermeasures are also modeled.
