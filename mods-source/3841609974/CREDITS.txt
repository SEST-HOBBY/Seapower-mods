Beriev Be-12 Chayka (NATO: Mail) for Sea Power, version 1.2 (21 Nov 2026)
Mod ID: sov_be12 (units sov_be12 and sov_be12n)

Model, textures and configuration: Thomas, built with Claude (Anthropic).
The aircraft model is original work, built by script from published three-view drawings and reference pictures.
No third-party mesh or texture is included. The paint, side numbers 20 to 32, red stars and weathering are drawn for this mod. The Vietnamese scheme (side number 1226) is drawn after a published colour profile of a Vietnamese Be-12.

Uses base-game content by reference (not included in this mod):
- sensors: Mushroom, SPO-3, SPO-10, WP_MAD
- weapons: wp_at-1, wp_at-2, wp_plab-100, wp_rgb-1, wp_rgb-nm1
- the Il-38 propeller disc material, sounds, effects, pilot figure and chaff
Set-up structure (bomb bay, MAD, sonobuoys, chaff) follows the base game's Il-38.

Data: Wikipedia (English and Russian) and other public sources on the Be-12. Radar, magnetometer and some performance figures are
gameplay estimates where no public figure exists.
