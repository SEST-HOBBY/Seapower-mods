[h1]THE REDFOR MOD: OTHER SIDE OF IRON CURTAIN[/h1]
[h1]Description[/h1]
[quote]This mod aim at bringing lesser known variants of vanilla units or new obscure units, and prototypes that almost entered serivice from 1980s to 2020s using extensive research in bid to make them as close to real life performance as possbile, this will include ships, submarines and ground units mainly from Russia and China. Biggest highlight of this mod right now is high fidelity S-300,S-400 SAM units with its dedicated radars and missiles and Iskander complex. But its containe many other interesting units for you to try too![/quote]
