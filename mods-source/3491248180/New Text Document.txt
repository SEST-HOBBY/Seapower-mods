[h2]The Royal Navy v0.4.19.0[/h2]

[b]Step back into the tense waters of the Cold War with this ship mod pack, bringing the might of the Royal Navy to Sea Power: Naval Combat in the Missile Age. From guided missile destroyers to nimble frigates, this mod recreates Britain’s seaborne power projection during a time when every sonar ping and radar blip could spark global conflict. Command a fleet forged in the shadow of the Iron Curtain and test your mettle in a high-stakes maritime chess match.[/b]
