02/09/25

 - Added Thomson CSF type BF RWR to etendard IVP, Super Etendard.
 - Added Thomson CSF TMV-011 Sherloc RWR to Super Etendard Modernisé.

IoT reflect the advanced nature of Sherloc for its time (digital RWR with wide frequency range, threat range and azimuth capabilities with threat library and type classification with discrimination between search radars and FCRs -  state of the art for the 80s), it was added in two flavours : RWR to warn of activated FCRs and ESM to reflect its capabilities in threat classification and range/azimuth.
The info on BF and Sherlock comes from two books on etendard and mirage F1 (iraqi mirages and handbrake! on super etendard in argentinian service).
