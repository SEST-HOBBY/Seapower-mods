Steelfin

Adds the Anduril Steelfin air defence interceptor, a Steelfin cruiser and a stationary container launcher paired with an air search radar. Includes an air-launched store for compatible aircraft loadouts.

Enable Steelfin in the mod manager and restart the game. The land and naval units appear under United States. Keep the land battery radar active for targets beyond visual range.

With the matching aircraft mods installed, select the Steelfin loadout on Fury Escort, Interceptor, Strike or SEAD, F-16 Block 70, either F-16XL, YF-23, XQ-58A, B-21 or either MV-75. Their existing loadouts remain available.

Steelfin is under development. This mod depicts future land, naval and air defence configurations. Detailed performance specifications have not been published.
