YF-23 Black Widow II

A fictional carrier-capable YF-23 with five combat loadouts:
Air to Air: six AIM-120Ds and two AIM-9X Block IIs.
Air To Air Intercept: two AIM-424 Malice missiles.
SEAD: two AGM-88A HARMs and two AIM-9X Block IIs.
Strike Precision: two GBU-12s and two AIM-9X Block IIs.
Anti Ship: two AGM-84D Harpoons.

The 20 mm cannon and countermeasures remain available with every loadout.
The anti-ship loadout uses the full bay and carries no air-to-air missiles.
Empty and Ferry are also available. Select the loadout in the mission editor
or when preparing aircraft for launch.

Six short scenarios are in YF-23 Loadout Trials. Each starts airborne with
one target, clear weather and no supporting mods required. Southern Watch
contains four longer scenarios.

The operational aircraft and its weapon integration are fictional.
Requires only Sea Power. This version replaces the separate decade aircraft.
Existing saves containing those retired aircraft require the previous version.

The Intercept loadout uses the full bay and carries no Sidewinders. AIM-424
uses the published external dimensions and a clean finish without test tracking marks. Motor and
seeker performance are approximations. Its 255 nm nominal launch setting
is not a guaranteed engagement range; altitude and target motion matter.
The long-range trial starts at 180 nm.

Intercept adds a dedicated long-range targeting mode for Malice. The other
four loadouts retain their existing sensors. The map circle shows a maximum
envelope; altitude, target direction, detection and remaining energy still
affect firing range. Motor flame starts after the missile clears the bay.
Twin exhaust trails now originate at the YF-23 exhaust outlets.
