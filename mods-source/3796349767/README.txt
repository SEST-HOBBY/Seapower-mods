YF-23 Black Widow II

A carrier capable YF-23 imagined for US Navy service, with five combat loadouts:
Air to Air: six AIM-120Ds and two AIM-9X Block IIs.
Air To Air Intercept: two AIM-424 Malice missiles with a dedicated long range targeting mode.
SEAD: two AGM-88A HARMs and two AIM-9X Block IIs.
Strike Precision: two GBU-12s and two AIM-9X Block IIs.
Anti Ship: two AGM-84D Harpoons.

Intercept and Anti Ship fill the whole bay and carry no Sidewinders. Every loadout, Ferry included, keeps the 20 mm cannon and countermeasures. Empty and Ferry are also available. Pick the loadout in the mission editor or when preparing aircraft for launch.

AIM-424 uses its published external dimensions; motor and seeker performance are estimates. The 255 nm launch setting and the map circle show the maximum envelope, while altitude, target motion, detection and remaining energy decide the real firing range. The motor lights once the missile clears the bay, and twin exhaust trails start at the engine outlets.



Requires Sea Power and Alpine Mod Loader; see START HERE.txt. This version replaces the separate decade aircraft, so saves containing those retired aircraft need the previous version.
